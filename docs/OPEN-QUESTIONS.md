# Open questions for the site team and installing contractor

**Please flag wrong or outdated items.** Questions below are not instructions to change valves, wiring or controls.

**Current basis:** [24–27 November team brief](CURRENT-TEAM-BRIEF-2026-11-24.md). P5.2 remains installed; the temporary module has not been released for operation.

1. **Preserving the existing plant:** Are the temporary connection points and the functions that must remain untouched clearly identified? P5.2 is to stay installed.
2. **Current useful cooling:** Which P2 / P3 / Alfa / Güntner functions are providing useful cooling during pressing? Are there time-aligned existing measurements of flow, temperatures and operating states?
3. **Independent temporary circulation:** Which two pump duties and connections are actually required without removing P5.2 or sacrificing useful existing freecooling?
4. **Heat exchanger and pumps:** Which real process and outdoor liquid duties are required? Can components already on site meet those duties, or must rated equipment be supplied?
5. **Temporary water reserve:** Is the reported 600 L container usable, are the three incoming 1,000 L IBCs actually received/clean/fillable, and where does compatible cold water come from?
6. **Winter conditions:** What is the accepted protection against freezing, empty tanks, loss of flow, leaks and power loss? If drainage is proposed, how are the tanks and residual liquid in pumps, valves and the HX addressed and verified before freezing conditions?
7. **Production trial:** What would count as a repeatable test for the two presses and final record quality, rather than a short period of lower basin temperature?
8. **What did we miss?** Please identify any piece of the actual system, existing pump, heat sink or control dependency missing from this overview.

When correcting an item, indicate **what you personally observed**, **what is historical documentation**, and **what is a suggestion**. Please send confidential evidence privately, not in public GitHub issues.


## Added closure questions — 24 November

1. What does the applicable factory manual permit in the complete hot process-water/steam/return path, and what fluid is actually present?
2. Does each selected pump meet its manufacturer's inlet requirements at the lowest working liquid level?
3. How are internal HX leakage and contamination detected and handled?
4. Where is the separate empty recovery capacity, and how is every exposed water volume protected after a single-pump failure or total outage?
5. What is the buffer exhaustion criterion, even if both pumps and flow indicators remain healthy?
6. How do instrument accuracy, mounting delay and actuator response affect the protective margins?
7. Who owns the bounded commissioning procedure, measured operating release and each end-of-shift handover?
